3. What we collect

When you ask for a free case file:

  • Your email address (required).
  • Your first name, if you choose to give it.
  • Which case you signed up for. This is recorded as a tag, for example case-montgomery, so we can send the right file.
  • Whether you ticked the box for the weekly Case Files email.
  • A consent record. Our email provider keeps the date and time, the page you signed up on, your IP address, and whether you confirmed your address. We keep this to prove you asked to hear from us.

When you read our emails:

  • Kit records whether you open an email and which links you click, using standard tracking pixels and tracked links.
  • We use this to see which case files people find useful, and to stop emailing people who never open them.

When you reply to us: we receive your email and whatever you choose to write. For example, your vote on the next mystery, or a correction.

When you buy a Complete Edition:

  • Gumroad processes the payment. We never see or store your card or bank details.
  • Gumroad shares with us:
  • your email address;
  • your name, if you give it;
  • what you bought, the price, and the date;
  • your country, and possibly your postal code for tax purposes.

What we do not collect:

  • We do not ask for sensitive information.
  • We do not buy email lists.
  • We do not put advertising pixels on our signup pages. That means no Meta pixel and no Google Ads tags.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as California law defines that term.

We use these service providers, and only to run the Case Files:

  • Kit (Kit, formerly ConvertKit, a US company). Hosts our signup pages, stores the email list, sends our emails and records consent. See Kit's privacy policy at https://kit.com/privacy.
  • Gumroad (Gumroad, Inc., a US company). Runs checkout as Merchant of Record: Gumroad is the seller of record for tax purposes and handles payments, sales tax, VAT and GST. For payment and tax data, Gumroad acts under its own privacy policy, https://gumroad.com/privacy. Gumroad's payment processors handle card data. We never receive it.
  • Our domain registrar and DNS provider (Namecheap) for the short links on vibewithjudeo.com. These are redirects only.
  • Professional advisers or authorities, if the law requires it, for example a valid legal request.

6. International transfers

Kit and Gumroad store data in the United States. If you are in the EU, UK or Canada, your information will be processed outside your country.

Where EU or UK law applies, we rely on the safeguards these providers offer, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum) or certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension.

Under Canadian law, information processed in another country may be accessible to that country's courts and authorities.

7. Cookies

  • Our signup pages (hosted by Kit) may use cookies or similar technology to load the page, protect the form from spam, and remember that you've already signed up. We do not add advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • Gumroad checkout uses its own cookies to run the checkout and prevent fraud, under Gumroad's policy.
  • YouTube sets its own cookies when you watch our videos, under Google's policy. Clicking a link in a video description takes you off YouTube.
  • vibewithjudeo.com short links only redirect you. We don't set cookies on them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser. The signup form may not work without the cookies it needs.

9. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy (access);
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information. We'll keep only what the law makes us keep, such as tax records or a suppression entry;
  • stop emailing you. Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to us. We act on unsubscribes immediately, and always within 10 business days, as CASL requires;
  • withdraw your consent at any time. This doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the rights to:

  • object;
  • restrict processing;
  • data portability.

If you are in California, you have the rights to know, delete and correct, and the right not to be discriminated against for using them. We do not sell or share personal information as California law defines it. Some of these laws apply only to larger businesses, but we extend the same rights to everyone.

How to ask:

  • Email hello@vibewithjudeo.com from the address on our list.
  • We may ask you to confirm it's you.
  • We'll reply within 30 days. Canadian law, and in most cases GDPR, requires a reply within about one month.
  • It's free.

Complaints: please tell us first, and we'll try to fix it. You can also contact:

  • Canada (federal): Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, priv.gc.ca
  • British Columbia: Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for BC, oipc.bc.ca
  • UK: Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk
  • EU: your national data protection authority
  • California: California Privacy Protection Agency, cppa.ca.gov
